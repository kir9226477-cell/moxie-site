Privacy Policy

Updated 9 November 2026

Moxie is a motivation app built around a daily mood check-in. It works without signing up and collects only what it needs to pick ideas for you.

What stays on your phone

Your date of birth and your faith (if you choose to share it) never leave your device. Moxie uses them only to work out your zodiac sign, your age group and the tone of your messages. Your notification schedule and daily plans are built and stored on the phone.

What is stored in your iCloud

A copy of your profile, rhythm settings, mood history and saved ideas is kept in your own iCloud (Apple's key-value storage), so a new iPhone with the same Apple ID can pick up where you left off. We cannot read your iCloud.

What is stored on our server

To back up your data and keep the service working, the app sends the following to our database, hosted by Supabase: your first name, zodiac sign, age group, goals, the optional work and relationship details you choose to add, your rhythm settings, the ideas you save and your mood check-ins (good / okay / bad and the date). This is linked to a random anonymous ID created by the app — there is no account unless you sign in. Access is restricted at the database level: only your own ID can read or change your records.

Sign in with Apple (optional)

If you choose to sign in, we receive your Apple account identifier and your email address — including Apple's private relay address if you hide your email. We use it only to restore your data on another device. The app never sees your Apple ID password.

Subscriptions

Payments are processed by Apple. We receive only the status of your subscription, never your payment details.

What we do not do

No ads, no third-party analytics, no tracking across apps or websites, no advertising identifiers. We do not sell or share your data. We do not access your location, contacts or photos.

Notifications

All notifications are scheduled on your phone. Answers you give inside a notification are stored on the phone and then synced as described above.

Deleting your data

In the app: You → Delete my data. This erases your data from the phone, from your iCloud and from our server, immediately and permanently. You can also email us to request deletion.

Not medical advice

Moxie is a motivation app, not therapy or a medical service. It does not diagnose or treat any condition.

Age

Moxie is intended for adults (18+) and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address and the date above will change.
