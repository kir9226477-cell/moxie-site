Support

Questions, ideas or something not working? Write to ruslankir317@gmail.com — we usually reply within two days.

Notifications don't arrive

Check Settings → Notifications → Moxie is allowed, and that a Focus mode (Sleep, Do Not Disturb) isn't hiding them at your morning time. Opening Moxie once a day keeps the schedule fresh.

How do I answer inside the notification?

Press and hold the Moxie notification (or swipe it down on the lock screen). Good, Okay and Bad appear underneath.

New phone — how do I keep my history?

Use the same Apple ID with iCloud on, or sign in with Apple in Moxie (You → Account). Your profile and mood history come back automatically.

Cancel a subscription

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Moxie. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.

Delete my data

In the app: You → Delete my data. It removes everything from the phone, your iCloud and our server.
